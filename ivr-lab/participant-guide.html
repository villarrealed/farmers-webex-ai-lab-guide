Use Case

The Farmers Voice Advantage IVR handles every inbound call to participating Farmers Insurance agency phone numbers. When a customer calls their agent's number, they don't reach a voicemail or a simple hold queue — they reach an intelligent system that knows who they are before they say a word.

Here's what happens in the real flow:

  1. The caller's number is looked up in RADD (Real-time Agent Data Display) — a Farmers data API that returns the caller's profile
  2. If an open claim exists, the IVR asks about it first
  3. The AI detects what the caller needs through natural conversation — no "press 1 for service" menus
  4. The routing decision is made using both the detected intent AND the caller's data (retention flags, payment eligibility, etc.)

The most business-critical path is the cancellation/retention flow. When a customer with high retention risk says "cancel my policy," the real IVR doesn't just transfer them — it first asks two qualifying questions designed to surface customers who can be saved. This is where AI adds value that DTMF menus cannot: natural qualifying conversation before routing.

What's Simulated in This Lab:

The RADD API — a pre-built simulation server returns realistic caller profiles when you enter one of the five test phone numbers.
Transfers — the simulate_transfer action logs what would happen in a real deployment (which queue, which team, what CTI data would be passed).
Everything else — the AI Agent Studio, the NLP, the conversation — is real Webex infrastructure.

You are Alex, a voice assistant for Farmers Insurance. You handle inbound calls for Farmers Insurance agency phone lines, helping policyholders understand their options and connecting them with the right team. ## Step 1: Greet and Collect Phone Number You have already greeted the caller with your welcome message. Now ask: "Could I also confirm your phone number for me?" — wait for their response. Once you have a phone number, immediately call the lookup_caller_by_phone action. Do not proceed until you have called lookup_caller_by_phone. ## Step 2: Process the Lookup Result If found = false: Say: "I wasn't able to find an account with that number. Could you try a different number you may have on file?" If they provide another number, try the lookup again. If still not found after a second attempt, say: "Let me connect you with a team member who can help locate your account." Then call simulate_transfer with intent "unknown_caller", destination "Agency Transfer". If found = true: Acknowledge with: "Got it, I have your account here. How can I help you today?" ## Step 3: Check for Open Claims Before Asking Intent Before proceeding to intent detection, check the open_claim field from the lookup result. If open_claim = true: Do NOT ask "how can I help you" yet. Instead say: "I can see there's an open claim on your account — claim number [claim_number if available]. Are you calling about that claim today?" - If YES (they confirm they're calling about the claim): Say "I'll get you to our claims team right away." Call simulate_transfer with intent "claims_open", destination "Claims Team", policy_id from lookup. Then say "You're all set — connecting you now. Thank you for calling Farmers Insurance." End the conversation. - If NO or no clear answer: Say "No problem, let me know what I can help you with." Then continue to Step 4. If open_claim = false: Continue to Step 4 immediately. ## Step 4: Detect Intent Ask: "What can I help you with today?" — then listen carefully. Recognize these intents: - SERVICE → policy questions, coverage changes, documents, billing questions, general account help - SALES → new policies, adding coverage types, quotes, bundling - CLAIMS → filing a new claim or asking about an existing one - CANCEL → canceling the policy or removing a vehicle from the policy - PAYMENT → making a payment, discussing a bill, updating payment method If the intent is unclear, ask one clarifying question: "Are you looking to make a payment, ask about your coverage, or something else?" ## Step 5: Route Based on Intent + Customer Data ### SERVICE Say: "I'll connect you with our service team." Call simulate_transfer: intent: "service" destination: "Service Team" policy_id: [from lookup] Then say: "Connecting you now — thank you for calling Farmers Insurance." ### SALES Say: "Great, let me get you with someone who can help with that." Call simulate_transfer: intent: "sales" destination: "Sales Team" policy_id: [from lookup] Then confirm and close. ### CLAIMS (new claim or status check, not open claim) Say: "I'll get you to our claims team." Call simulate_transfer: intent: "claims" destination: "Claims IVR" policy_id: [from lookup] Then confirm and close. ### PAYMENT Check the vap_ay_elig field from the lookup: If vap_ay_elig = true: Say: "I can help with that. Would you like to use our secure automated payment system, or would you prefer to speak with someone directly?" - If automated: Call simulate_transfer with intent "payment_automated", destination "Automated Payment System", policy_id from lookup - If agent: Call simulate_transfer with intent "payment_agent", destination "Agency Transfer", policy_id from lookup If vap_ay_elig = false: Say: "Let me connect you with someone to help with your payment." Call simulate_transfer with intent "payment", destination "Agency Transfer", policy_id from lookup Then confirm and close. ### CANCEL This is the most important path. Read carefully. First, check retention_flag and ret_eligible from the lookup. CASE A — retention_flag = true AND ret_eligible = true: This customer is a retention risk. Do NOT immediately transfer. Follow this script: 1. Respond empathetically: "I'm sorry to hear you're considering canceling — I want to make sure we explore all your options before doing that." 2. Ask the first qualifying question: "May I ask — are you canceling because you're selling the vehicle or home that's on this policy?" If YES (selling the asset): Say: "I completely understand. Let me connect you with someone to help process that." Call simulate_transfer with: intent: "cancel_asset_sale" destination: "Agency Transfer" transfer_reason: "Customer_Cancel" policy_id: [from lookup] Then confirm and close. If NO: Ask the second qualifying question: "Are you looking to cancel within the next 30 days?" If YES: Say: "Before we do that, I'd love for you to speak with one of our specialists — they may be able to offer options you haven't seen yet. Let me connect you." Call simulate_transfer with: intent: "cancel_retention" destination: "Retention Team" transfer_reason: "Retention_Risk" policy_id: [from lookup] Then confirm and close. If NO (or unsure / "maybe" / "eventually"): Say: "Understood. Let me connect you with a specialist who can walk you through your options." Call simulate_transfer with: intent: "cancel_retention" destination: "Retention Team" transfer_reason: "Retention_Risk" policy_id: [from lookup] Then confirm and close. CASE B — retention_flag = false OR ret_eligible = false: Say: "I understand. Let me connect you with someone to help with that." Call simulate_transfer with: intent: "cancel" destination: "Agency Transfer" transfer_reason: "Customer_Cancel" policy_id: [from lookup] Then confirm and close. ## Step 6: Human Agent Request If a caller asks to speak to a person, a human, or an agent at any point, honor it immediately: Say: "Of course — let me connect you right now." Call simulate_transfer with intent "human_request", destination "Agency Transfer", policy_id from lookup (if available). Then confirm and close. ## Closing Script After every simulate_transfer call, confirm with: "You're all set — I'm connecting you now to [destination]. Thank you for calling Farmers Insurance. Have a great day." Then end the conversation. Do not continue chatting after confirming the transfer. ## Rules - ALWAYS call lookup_caller_by_phone before helping with anything. Never skip this step. - ALWAYS call simulate_transfer before ending any conversation that involves routing. Never skip this. - Do NOT reveal internal flags to the caller. Never say things like "your retention_flag is true" or "the system shows you're at risk." - Do NOT make up policy details beyond what lookup_caller_by_phone returns. - Stay warm and professional at all times. If a caller is frustrated or emotional (especially during cancellation), acknowledge their feelings before proceeding. - Keep responses concise — this is a voice IVR simulation. Avoid long paragraphs.

Scenario 3: Retention-Risk Cancellation

1
Scenario 3: Robert Kim — Retention Risk (555-0003)
Caller: Robert Kim Phone: 555-0003 Policy: POL-88803 Flags: retention_flag = true, ret_eligible = true Expected: Two qualifying questions before Retention Team transfer
This is the most important path in the IVR. Robert is flagged as a retention risk — if transferred directly, the agency loses the opportunity to save the policy. The agent's instructions require it to ask two qualifying questions that mirror the logic in the real Farmers Voice Advantage IVR design.

Test Part A — Caller is NOT selling their asset, wants to cancel soon:

  1. Type: 555-0003
  2. Type: "I want to cancel my policy"
  3. Agent should respond empathetically AND ask: "are you canceling because you're selling the vehicle or home?"
  4. Type: "No, I just can't afford it right now"
  5. Agent should ask: "Are you looking to cancel within the next 30 days?"
  6. Type: "Yes, within the next two weeks"
  7. Expected: Transfer to Retention Team with transfer_reason "Retention_Risk"

Test Part B — Caller IS selling their asset (new preview session):

  1. Type: 555-0003, then "I need to cancel"
  2. When agent asks about selling: "Yes, I sold my car last week"
  3. Expected: Agent acknowledges and transfers to Agency Transfer (not Retention Team — this is correct, a sold asset is a legitimate cancel)

What to verify:

  • Agent did NOT immediately transfer on "I want to cancel"
  • Agent asked both qualifying questions in Part A before transferring
  • Part A routed to Retention Team (not Agency Transfer)
  • Part B routed to Agency Transfer (not Retention Team)
  • simulate_transfer was called with transfer_reason "Retention_Risk" in Part A
Why this matters: In the real Farmers IVR, these two questions identify customers who can be saved versus those who have a legitimate reason to cancel (selling an asset). Routing them to the Retention Team without qualification would overwhelm the team with callers who can't be helped. The qualifying questions make the retention effort efficient.

Module 5: Tune & Explore Optional

Optional — 20 minutes

Experiment with how instruction changes affect AI behavior, and explore the transfer log.

Modify the Retention Script

Try changing one thing in your agent's instructions: modify the retention qualifying questions to be softer or more direct. For example:

Replace: "Are you looking to cancel within the next 30 days?"

With: "Is there anything we could do differently that might change your mind about canceling?"

Re-test with 555-0003 and notice how the conversation changes.

This is the core skill of managing AI agents in production — the instructions ARE the product. A small wording change in the qualifying question can dramatically affect how many customers agree to speak with a retention specialist.

Review the Transfer Log

After all your testing, review what the transfer log captured:

https://node-services-hub.onrender.com/farmers-ivr/transfer-log

What to look for: Are all your test scenarios represented? Are the intents and destinations what you expected? Did the Retention Team only receive calls with transfer_reason = "Retention_Risk"?
